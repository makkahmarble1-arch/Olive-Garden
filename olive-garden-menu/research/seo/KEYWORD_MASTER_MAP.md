## Notes on wording choices
- The URL slugs stay exactly as approved, even where the searched phrase differs:
  - `/kids-meal` vs "kids menu".
  - `/gluten-sensitive` vs "gluten free".
  - `/non-alcoholic-beverages` vs "non alcoholic drinks".
  - `/sides-sauces-and-extras` vs "sides".

  Titles and H1s can carry the searched phrase.
- **Gluten:** use "gluten free" as the search phrase, but the page must state that Olive Garden calls these items **gluten-sensitive** (official wording; see `categories/gluten-sensitive.md`).
- **Year modifiers** ("2026") appear in many observed suggestions. Use them only if the page is genuinely re-checked for that year.
- **"pdf" modifiers** are frequent (menu pdf, catering pdf, gluten free pdf). We do not publish menu PDFs. Pages can link to official PDFs where they exist (nutrition, allergen guide).
- **Recipe and copycat queries** (alfredo sauce recipe, breadsticks recipe, sangria recipe) are a different intent and are not targeted.
