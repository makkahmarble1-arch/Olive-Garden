# Content Gaps (Lesson 8.1)

**Research date:** 2026-11-13.

These are opportunities that competitors on page 1 miss and that our verified data (`research/menu/`, official Millenia online pickup prices, checked 2026-11-12) can support. Only relevant gaps are listed per page.

- **Calculated** = derived from official values and labeled as calculated on the page.
- Nothing here is an estimate.

## Site-wide gaps (apply to every category page)
1. **Reference location + order type + exact check date** on every price table. No competitor shows all three.
2. **Calories with units** (per item, per bundle, per bottle). Competitors often show none or an unsourced range.
3. **Status dates for limited-time items and promotions**: lunch offer, lighter portions, Spicy Alfredo, Pumpkin Cookie Butter Cheesecake, Buy One Take One.
4. **Correct official wording**: gluten-sensitive, (V), "serves 4-6".
5. **Genuine visitor ratings** answer "best" questions without invented rankings.

## Questions competitors leave unanswered (FAQ candidates, per page)
- **Buy One Take One:** Is it available today? What replaced it? (date-stamped)
- **Lighter vs lunch:** What is the actual difference? (calories equal, price and time differ)
- **Soup and salad:** Is the $9.99 price available on weekends? (no; weekday until 3 PM)
- **Lunch:** Is lunch served on Saturday or Sunday? (no; lighter portions instead)
- **Kids:** Do kids eat free? What age counts?
- **Catering:** How much do I order for 50 people? Is there a delivery minimum?
- **Gluten:** Is anything certified gluten-free? (only the rotini meets the FDA definition; items are gluten-sensitive)
- **Wine:** Can I buy a bottle to go, and what does it cost?

## Not recommended (out of scope or unsupported)
- Copycat recipes (strong in autocomplete, different intent).
- Coupon codes, "kids eat free" claims, cocktail prices, refill policy: no official source verified yet.
- Menu PDF downloads of our own.
- "Lawsuit" topics (gluten): no verified source.
- Any health, weight-loss, or allergy-safety claims.
