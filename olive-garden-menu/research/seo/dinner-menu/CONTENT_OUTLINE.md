## Outline
1. **H1 + intro.** There is no separate dinner menu; when dinner applies; entrée range $13.99-$26.49; soup or salad and breadsticks included. Facts bar: 7 menu sections, last verified (month and year), online pickup, USA, prices vary.
2. **H2 Olive Garden Dinner Prices at a Glance.** Price overview table, calculated from the shared data: items, price range, calories, and when each section is served. Then **H3 Dinner Picks with Photos**: five premium menu cards with shared ratings (the owner's popular items, presented as our picks, not a ranking).
3. **H2 Olive Garden Dinner Hours.** Weekday 3 PM switch, weekends and holidays all day, closing times (restaurant we checked), online ordering cutoff, holiday closures, regional variation; link to the homepage hours section.
4. **H2 Dinner Appetizers.** Range and examples; link to `/appetizers`.
5. **H2 Classic Dinner Entrées.** Price ladder table (11 rows); lowest, highest, and lowest-calorie plates; links to `/classic-entrees` and `/create-your-own-pasta`.
6. **H2 Alfredo Dishes at Dinner.** Five dishes with prices, Spicy Alfredo, calories, lighter option; link to `/amazing-alfredos`.
7. **H2 Soup, Salad and Breadsticks at Dinner.** What is included; dinner $13.49 vs weekday lunch $9.99 table; link to `/soups-salad-and-breadsticks`.
8. **H2 Smaller Plates at Dinner: Lighter Portions.** When served, price range, three regular-vs-lighter examples; link to `/lighter-portions`.
9. **H2 Dinner Desserts and Drinks.** Ranges, lightest and richest dessert, birthday dessert, drinks, cocktails, wine and beer; links to `/desserts`, `/drink-menu`, `/wine-and-beer-to-go`.
10. **H2 Lunch vs Dinner: What Changes at 3 PM.** Six verified differences; link to the dish-by-dish table on `/lunch-menu`. The heading differs from the lunch page's "Olive Garden Lunch vs Dinner Menu".
11. **H2 How to Save Money at Dinner.** Example orders table (6 rows, calculated); five verified tips; link to `/buy-one-take-one`.
12. **H2 Frequently Asked Questions.** 8 questions (see `KEYWORD_RESEARCH.md` §6).
13. **Sources** box: Olive Garden website + FAQs; month and year.
14. **Related Menus:** Classic Entrees, Lunch, Lighter Portions, Alfredo, Appetizers, Desserts, Drinks, Family-Style Meals.

## Changes from the brief (adjusted to the research)
- **"Popular Dinner Picks"** became photo cards inside the price section ("Dinner Picks with Photos"). There is no official popularity data, so the page does not rank dishes.
- **Added "Smaller Plates at Dinner: Lighter Portions":** a dinner-time-only menu section that competitors miss.
- **No full item tables** for entrées, appetizers, or desserts. They live on the category pages (no duplication); this page shows ranges, a price ladder, and links.
- **Lunch vs dinner** is a short list plus a link, because `/lunch-menu` owns the dish-by-dish table.
