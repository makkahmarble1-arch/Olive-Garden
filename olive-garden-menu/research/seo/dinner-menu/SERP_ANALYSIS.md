## 2. Competitor patterns
- Most pages answer "dinner menu" with the **whole menu**, unchanged from their main menu page. There is no dinner angle: no hours, no lunch-vs-dinner rule, no dinner-for-two cost.
- **Hours:** the common answer is "dinner starts at 3 PM". Few pages say that the dinner menu is served all day on weekends and holidays, even though Olive Garden's FAQ says so.
- **Prices:** ranges are vague or contradictory ("$11.99-$16.49", "$13.99-$18.99 for spaghetti", "$16-$23 typical entrées"). Most are undated or cite other locations.
- **Lunch vs dinner:** food media compare a few dishes (lasagna, Alfredo) with third-party prices. They often mention the old "Lunch Duos" and miss the 2026 lighter portions.
- **Specials:** pages still list the "2 for $25" dinner (a 2012-era promotion) or Buy One Take One without saying it has ended.

## 3. Content gaps we fill
1. A clear answer that there is **no separate dinner menu**, and exactly when the dinner menu applies (weekdays from 3 PM; all day on weekends and holidays), with the official source.
2. **Price ranges for every part of the dinner menu**, computed from verified data with the month and year.
3. **Lighter portions as the dinner-time small plate**, a 2026 change that most competitors miss.
4. **Calculated example orders** (budget, lighter, classic, with dessert, with $6 Take Home), food only, labeled "Calc.".
5. An honest **dinner specials** answer (no dinner-only special found in November 2026; $6 Take Home and the limited-time Pasta Bowl apply).
6. A short **lunch vs dinner** summary, linking to the existing dish-by-dish table instead of copying it.
