## 3. Semantic cluster [Inferred]
- **Time:** dinner starts at 3 PM on weekdays, all day on Saturday, Sunday, and holidays; closing time per restaurant; online ordering closes 20 minutes before closing (restaurant we checked).
- **Price:** dinner entrée range, appetizer, dessert, and drink ranges; dinner for two; regular vs lighter vs lunch price.
- **Menu terms:** classic entrées, Amazing Alfredos, Never-Ending first course (soup or salad and breadsticks), lighter portions, $6 Take Home, Never-Ending Pasta Bowl.
- **Comparison:** lunch vs dinner portions, size, and prices; what changes at 3 PM.

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and local. People want to know what they can order at dinner, what it costs, and when dinner starts.
- **Secondary:**
  - Hours ("dinner menu time", "what time does olive garden open for dinner").
  - Deals ("dinner specials today", "dinner deals").
  - Party size ("dinner for two").
  - Comparison with lunch (portions, size, prices).
- **Not targeted:** "pdf" (we publish no menu PDFs), "near me" (no store locator), "christmas dinner prices" (restaurants are closed on Christmas Day; seasonal catering is a different intent), and copycat recipes.
