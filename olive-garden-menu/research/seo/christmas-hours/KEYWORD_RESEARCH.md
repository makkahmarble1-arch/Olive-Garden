## 3. What ranks (2026-11-25), observed only

- **News roundups:** "Restaurants open on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day in 2025" (Fox stations), Axios 2025 list, The Daily Meal (2023), Heavy.
  - They answer in one line ("closed Christmas Day, closed early Christmas Eve") and are usually from last year.
- **Holiday-hours aggregators and look-alike "menu" sites:** everydayhours, eateryhours, storesinfo, several olivegardenmenu*.com/.us sites.
  - Many state exact Christmas Eve closing times (6 PM, 8 PM, or 9 PM) with no official source, and they contradict each other.
  - Some give wrong dates (for example, a Thanksgiving date of November 27 or 28 for 2026).
- **Official pages:** olivegarden.com/faqs ranks for the policy question but gives no 2026 dates and no restaurant times.

## 4. Content gaps we fill

- Correct **2026 dates with weekdays** (Thu Dec 24, Fri Dec 25, Sat Dec 26) and a three-day status view.
- A clear line between **what Olive Garden states** and what is **not announced**, instead of invented times.
- **To Go, delivery, and catering** answers for both days, including the next-day catering rule applied to Christmas Eve.
- Practical tips that send visitors to our own pages (family-style meals, desserts, dinner menu, catering).
