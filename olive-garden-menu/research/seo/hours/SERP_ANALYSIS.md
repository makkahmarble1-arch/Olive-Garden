- olive garden hours today / opening closing times / holiday hours guide

## 2. Competitor patterns
- **Typical hours:** usually "11 AM-10 PM Sunday-Thursday, 11 AM-11 PM Friday-Saturday", often without a source.
- **Holidays:** Thanksgiving and Christmas closures are widely reported. Other holidays get confident but unsourced answers; several pages invent reduced holiday hours.
- **Lunch and dinner times:** rarely tied to the official rule (lunch Monday-Friday until 3 PM; dinner menu all day on weekends and holidays).
- **To Go and catering cutoffs:** not covered.
- **How to check local hours:** usually a vague "check the website".
