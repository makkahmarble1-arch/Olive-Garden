## 3. Semantic cluster [Inferred]
- **Times:** opening time 11 AM, closing time 10 PM or 11 PM, Friday and Saturday hours, Sunday hours, hours of operation.
- **Menu times:** lunch until 3 PM on weekdays, dinner menu from 3 PM and all day on weekends and holidays.
- **Holidays:** Thanksgiving and Christmas Day closures, the day before Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, New Year's Day, Easter, Memorial Day, July 4, Labor Day, Halloween, Veterans Day.
- **Service:** takeout (To Go), online ordering cutoff, delivery, catering delivery.
- **Local:** restaurant locator, "near me", city/state/ZIP search, restaurant page, call the restaurant.

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** local and informational. People want a quick answer ("is it open now / today / on this holiday, and until when?"), usually on a phone.
- **"Near me" and "today":** the real answer is location-specific. We give the common pattern and send people to the official locator. We do not request location or show an "open now" status.
- **Holiday queries** are the most repeated modifier. They need clear "closed / may close early / not announced" answers, not invented times.
