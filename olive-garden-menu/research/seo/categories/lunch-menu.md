## 3. Semantic cluster [Inferred]
- **Timing and pricing:** weekday lunch, Monday-Friday 11 AM-3 PM, not on weekends or holidays, lunch prices $9.99-$12.49.
- **Menu terms:** Lunch-Sized Favorites, lunch portions vs lighter portions, comes with soup or salad and breadsticks, lunch sandwiches, flatbreads, soup-salad-breadsticks $9.99 lunch offer.
- **Dishes:** Shrimp Scampi, Lasagna, Chicken Parmigiana.

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and local. Visitors want lunch prices, what is on the menu, and whether it is available today.
- **Secondary:** weekend availability (most repeated modifier), hours, calories, to go.
- **Format:**
  - An availability box (days and hours, with check date).
  - A lunch table (plates, sandwiches, flatbreads).
  - Calories.
  - A link to the soup-and-salad deal.
