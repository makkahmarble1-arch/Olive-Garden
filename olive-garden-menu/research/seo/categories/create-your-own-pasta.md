## 3. Semantic cluster [Inferred]
- **Builder parts:** pasta shapes (rigatoni, spaghetti, angel hair, gluten-free rotini), sauces (traditional marinara, five cheese marinara, creamy mushroom, meat sauce for an extra charge), toppings for an extra charge (meatballs, Italian sausage, crispy chicken fritta, grilled chicken, sautéed shrimp).
- **Other:** base price, calorie range 530-1,300, comes with soup or salad and breadsticks, (G) and (V) labels.
- **Related but separate:** catering Create Your Own Pasta Station (separate product); Never-Ending Pasta Bowl promotion.

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** informational and commercial. Visitors want to know how the builder works, what the options are, and what the final price and calories will be.
- **Secondary:** sauces list, gluten-free pasta option, catering station headcount (a different intent; link out).
- **Format:** a 3-step builder explanation followed by option tables (pasta, sauce, topping) with price add-on and calories.
- **Immediate:** base price, included vs paid options, calorie range.
