- **Portion terms:** smaller portion, lower price, lighter portion vs regular entrée vs lunch portion, calories per portion, includes soup or salad and breadsticks.

- **Dishes:** Chicken Parmigiana, Lasagna Classico, Fettuccine Alfredo, Five Cheese Ziti, Spaghetti & Meatballs, Cheese Ravioli, Eggplant Parmigiana, Spicy Alfredo (new).

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and informational. Visitors want to know what is on the lighter menu, what it costs, and how it compares with regular and lunch portions.
- **Secondary:** calories and "healthy"/low-calorie choices; when it is available.
- **Format:** a three-way comparison table (regular / lighter / lunch), with price, calories, and availability.
- **Immediate:** list, price, calories, availability window.

## 6. Content gaps (the strongest unique angle among the 17 pages)
Our official data (Millenia, checked 2026-11-12) shows the **lighter and lunch versions have the same calories**. They differ only in price and time window:

| Dish | Regular entrée | Lighter (Mon-Fri after 3 PM, Sat-Sun all day) | Lunch (Mon-Fri 11 AM-3 PM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lasagna Classico | $19.99 · 940 cal | $14.49 · 500 cal | $12.49 · 500 cal |
| Chicken Parmigiana | $21.29 · 1,020 cal | $14.49 · 630 cal | $12.49 · 630 cal |
| Fettuccine Alfredo | $17.49 · 1,220 cal | $13.49 · 920 cal | $11.49 · 920 cal |
| Eggplant Parmigiana | $18.79 · 1,070 cal | $13.49 · 660 cal | $11.49 · 660 cal |

- Say that matching calories *suggests* the same portion size. Do not claim identical grams unless an official source says so.
- Calculated savings vs the regular entrée, labeled as calculated.
- "Lower-calorie" wording only, with no health or weight-loss claims (see `research/homepage/nutrition-guide.md`).
