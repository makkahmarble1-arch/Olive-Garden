## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** informational and commercial. Visitors want to know which wines and beers Olive Garden sells and what bottles cost to go.
- **Secondary:** sweet wines (Roscato, Moscato, Confetti); dine-in glass prices (not available online); rules for buying alcohol to go.
- **Format:** a bottle table (wine, style, price, calories per bottle), a beer table, a rules box.
