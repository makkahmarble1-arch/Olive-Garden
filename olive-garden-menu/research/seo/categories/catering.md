## 3. Semantic cluster [Inferred]
- **Packages:** meal combinations (serve 8-14), Create Your Own Pasta Station (multiples of 10), individual pans (serve 4-6), large lasagna (up to 12).
- **Extras:** soups by the pan, salad and breadsticks, appetizers and desserts trays, beverages (gallons, 2-liter).
- **Pricing and delivery:** price per person, $100 delivery minimum, 10% delivery fee (5% above $500), order by 5 PM for next-day lunch, chafing kit, pickup vs delivery.
- **Occasions:** business, wedding, holiday party.

## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and transactional **planning**. Visitors want to know how much catering for N people costs and what to order.
- **Secondary:** event types (wedding, office, holiday), coupons, delivery rules.
- **Format:**
  - Packages table (serves, from price per person).
  - Pans table.
  - Delivery rules box.
  - A **headcount planner** built from official "serves" values (calculated, labeled).

## 6. Content gaps
- **Headcount planner** for the most-searched sizes (10, 20, 50, 100 people). Use the official "serves" values and prices, label the result as calculated, and keep the "prices vary" note.
- **Delivery rules** in one box (official): minimum, fee, order cutoff, chafing kit.
- Per-person "from" prices quoted exactly as official, with the asterisk conditions.
- Restaurant vs catering portion comparison for desserts and appetizers (e.g. Tiramisu slice vs tray), linking to those pages.
- **Family-Style vs Catering:** pans appear on both at the same price. Catering adds combinations, the station, delivery rules, and event sizes; link to `/family-style-meals` for home dinners.
- **Coupons and "Christmas catering prices":** say only what is official. No coupon codes.
