## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** transactional and informational. Visitors want to know whether the offer is available now, what it costs, and what they can choose.
- **Secondary:** rules, end date, portion size, reheating, calories.
- **Format:** a status box (date-stamped), an eligible-items table, conditions, and a "what changed" note.
- **Immediate:**
  - Is Buy One Take One available today? (Answer: not currently, per the official page on 2026-11-13.)
  - What exists instead? ($6 Take Home)
- **Next:**
  - Eligible items and their regular price.
  - Conditions (with an entrée purchase, no soup or salad).
  - Reheating questions (link to the official instructions if published; do not invent any).
