## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** informational, with high stakes (health). Visitors want to know what they can safely order.
- **Secondary:** prices of GF items; GF pasta details; celiac safety; dairy-free (a separate allergen; link to the official allergen guide only).
- **Format:**
  - A caution box (official wording paraphrased).
  - The GF item table with price and calories.
  - "How to order" steps.
  - What is not available (no GF breadsticks, if official).

## 6. Content gaps
- **Accurate wording** (most competitors get this wrong): gluten-sensitive ≠ gluten-free; only the rotini meets the FDA gluten-free definition; no separate fryer; the kitchen is not gluten-free. All of this comes from official sources (`research/homepage/nutrition-guide.md`).
- **Prices and calories** for all 10 GF items (official, Millenia): $7.49 kids to $22.79 salmon; 110-810 cal. The specialist blog has none.
- An order-safely checklist (official guidance: tell the server, no croutons or breadsticks).
- Kids GF options linked to `/kids-meal`.
- No medical advice; celiac visitors are pointed to the official allergen guide.
