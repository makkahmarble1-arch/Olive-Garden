## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and local. Visitors want the price, and whether the deal applies now (weekday or weekend, lunch or dinner).
- **Secondary:** calories per soup, salad, and breadstick; to-go availability; soup options.
- **Format:** a status/price box (regular vs limited-time lunch price, days and hours), soups table, calorie table.
- **Immediate:**
  - Price now.
  - Days and hours.
  - Unlimited refills: dine-in only?
  - Which soups.
