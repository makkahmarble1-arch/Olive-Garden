## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** informational and commercial. Visitors want the full appetizer list with prices.
- **Secondary:** "best" or ranked picks, calories, new items, catering trays.
- **Format:** a complete table, a few photos, and short notes.
- **Immediate:** name, price, calories, portion.
- **Next:** dipping sauces included, vegetarian labels, which appetizers exist as catering trays.
