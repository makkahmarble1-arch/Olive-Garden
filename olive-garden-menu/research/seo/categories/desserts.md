## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and informational. Visitors want to see all desserts with prices (often with pictures).
- **Secondary:**
  - Calories and allergens.
  - "Right now" / seasonal items.
  - Birthday dessert.
  - Catering trays.
- **Format:** a photo-led table (price, calories), a seasonal note, and a birthday and catering note.

## 6. Content gaps
- A **current vs limited-time** label (the Pumpkin Cookie Butter Cheesecake is limited time; date-stamped).
- A calorie range across desserts (470 Tiramisu to 980 Chocolate Lasagna, official), presented as a table rather than a ranking.
- **Birthday:** a complimentary dessert when dining in on your birthday (official FAQ). One sentence.
- **Catering dessert trays** vs restaurant slice (Tiramisu $9.79 slice vs $40.49 catering tray, official), linking to `/catering`.
- Allergen pointer to the official allergen guide (no allergen claims of our own).
