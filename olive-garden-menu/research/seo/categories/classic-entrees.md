## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and informational. Visitors want the dinner entrée list with prices and what each includes.
- **Secondary:**
  - Nutrition (calories, protein for Chicken Parmigiana).
  - "What comes with entrees" [Observed].
  - Vegetarian options.
- **Format:** a full table grouped by type (pasta, chicken, seafood, steak), followed by included-sides facts.
- **Immediate:** name, price, calories, what is included.
- **Next:**
  - Where a smaller (lighter or lunch) portion exists, with links.
  - Protein choices and their price.
  - Vegetarian labels.
