## 4. Search intent
- **Primary:** commercial and transactional. Visitors want to know what family meals cost and what comes in them before they order to go.
- **Secondary:** compare a bundle with a pan, see how many people each feeds, and find deals.
- **Expected format:** a price table with a "serves" column, then what is included.
- **Needed immediately:** the list of bundles and pans, with price, servings, and what is included (salad, breadsticks).
- **Useful next:**
  - Cost per person (calculated from the official price ÷ servings, and labeled as calculated).
  - Total calories per bundle.
  - Which items exist only as pans.
  - How to order.

## 6. Content gaps and opportunities
- A bundle vs pan table: what each includes and how many it serves (official).
- **Cost per person**, calculated and labeled (price ÷ maximum servings).
- Total calories per bundle, with the unit "per bundle (total)" made clear.
- Family pans that appear on the Catering menu at the same price (our data shows identical prices). Link to `/catering` for events and keep this page for households.
- Breadstick and salad add-on quantities.
