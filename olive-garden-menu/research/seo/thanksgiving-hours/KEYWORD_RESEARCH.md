## 3. What ranks (2026-11-25), observed only

- **2026 news lists:** KOCO (Oklahoma City restaurants open on Thanksgiving 2026), WJLA (open and closed in the DC area, Thanksgiving 2026), Yahoo/USA Today network ("Olive Garden to be closed for Thanksgiving"), and Time Out / IBTimes ("closing all 900 US locations").
  - They give a one-line answer; some frame the yearly policy as news.
- **Aggregators and look-alike menu sites:** they claim "regular hours" or "reduced hours" on Thanksgiving Eve, with no source. One lists the wrong 2026 date.
- **Official:** the FAQ (policy) and location pages, whose indexed copies show a "closed Thursday, November 26, reopen Friday, November 27" notice.

## 4. Content gaps we fill

- The 2026 dates with weekdays and a three-day status view (Wed, Thu, Fri).
- Honest "not announced" labels instead of invented Eve hours.
- To Go, delivery, and catering answers, including the practical point that Thanksgiving food from Olive Garden has to be picked up by Wednesday.
- No turkey or holiday-meal claims (none verified), unlike some competitors.
