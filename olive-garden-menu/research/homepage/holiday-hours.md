**Checked:** 2026-11-12

- Official FAQs, "Holiday Hours" and "hours of operation":
  https://www.olivegarden.com/faqs

  https://www.olivegarden.com/locations/fl/orlando/orlando-millenia-mall/1614

## What the official site confirms

1. Olive Garden restaurants are **closed on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas
   Day** (official FAQ). Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and international
   restaurants may differ; Orlando is not one of those exceptions.
2. On the **day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve**, restaurants may
   close early at the manager's discretion. The official advice is to call
   the restaurant for that day's closing time.
3. On **holidays the dinner menu is served all day** (no lunch menu), with
   "some regional variations" (official FAQ).
4. Hours vary by location; the official site sends diners to their nearest
   restaurant's page for hours.
