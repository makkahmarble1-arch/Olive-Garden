## Key official facts (paraphrased, not copied)

- **Calories:** the menu notes that 2,000 calories a day is used for general
  nutrition advice and that needs vary; more nutrition information is
  available on request. Values are based on standard recipes and can vary.
- **Entrée calories do not include the included first course.** Salad with
  dressing 150, soups 110-230 per serving, each breadstick 140 (PDF).
- **Gluten sensitive:** these items are made without gluten-containing
  ingredients but are **not** called gluten-free (not lab-tested to the FDA
  20 ppm standard). The gluten-free rotini is made mainly from brown rice
  flour, meets the FDA gluten-free definition, and is cooked and held
  separately. There is **no separate fryer**. Kitchens are not gluten-free;
  tell your server you are avoiding gluten (croutons and breadsticks are
  left off on request).
- **Allergens:** the allergen guide is based on supplier information. When
  you tell your server about an allergy, the order is flagged and the kitchen
  follows extra steps (for example, changing gloves and utensils).
- **Vegetarian / vegan:** Olive Garden defines vegetarian as no meat, stock,
  gelatin, or rennet from an animal, and vegan as also excluding
  animal-derived ingredients such as honey. Cross-contact with animal
  products is possible. 31 offerings carry the official (V) label.
- **Trans fat:** cooking oils contain no trans fat (official FAQ).

## Gluten-sensitive menu (official listing, Millenia)

Herb-Grilled Salmon (610), Zuppa Toscana (220), Famous House Salad without
croutons (110), Grilled Chicken Parmigiana with gluten-free rotini (810),
6 oz Sirloin (480), Rotini Pasta with Marinara (530) or Meat Sauce (680),
and three kids meals with gluten-free rotini (260-430).

## Wording rules

- Say "gluten-sensitive", never "gluten-free", except for the rotini pasta
  itself, as the official site does.
- No medical or weight-loss claims; link to the official allergen guide for
  allergy decisions.
