### Lunch-hours conflict: resolved (3 PM)

- Location page: lunch **11:00 AM - 3:00 PM, Monday-Friday**.
- Official specials page (https://www.olivegarden.com/specials/never-ending-soup-salad-and-breadsticks):
  Never-Ending Soup, Salad & Breadsticks **$9.99, Monday-Friday until 3 PM**,
  limited time, refills dine-in only.
- Lunch section item page: same meal "Available Monday-Friday, 11am-3pm".
- Only the Weekday Lunch Special card says "11am-2:30pm". Three official
  sources agree on 3 PM, so 3 PM is used.
- Official FAQ: "Lunch is served Monday through Friday until 3pm"; the dinner
  menu is served all day on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.
- **Suggested wording:** "Lunch is served Monday-Friday, 11 AM-3 PM. Hours can
  vary by location, so check with your restaurant."

## Location differences

- Hours, lunch periods, and online-ordering windows are set per restaurant.
  These hours are for the Orlando - Millenia Mall restaurant only.
- The official site shows hours only after a location is chosen; diners
  should check their own restaurant's page.
- Hours are shown for "today" and the next six days; they can change for
  holidays (see `holiday-hours.md`).
