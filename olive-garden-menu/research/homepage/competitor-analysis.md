# Homepage Research: Competitor Analysis

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Method:** web search for "Olive Garden menu with prices 2026" and
"Olive Garden menu prices calories" (official site excluded), then each
top result's page structure was read with a text fetcher.
**Limits:** Chrome was not connected, so **visual design (colors, layout,
mobile view, ad density) was not checked**. Only headings, sections, and
text claims were analyzed. No text, images, or data were copied. Nothing
from these sites is used as a source of prices or calories.

## 2. Common page pattern

Every reviewed page is one very long "full menu" page:

1. H1 with "Menu + Prices + 2026/month"
2. "Prices at a glance" summary (item count, cheapest item, category count)
3. Jump-link table of contents
4. One H2 + table per category (price, calories, sometimes protein/fat)
5. Deals / coupons / limited-time section
6. Nutrition lists (healthiest, highest protein, "best value")
7. Hours and holiday hours
8. Long FAQ (8-20 questions)
9. Comparison with other Italian chains
10. Huge footer link list to other restaurants' menus

Length: about 1,500 to 9,000 words per page.

## 4. Problems found on competitor pages

- **Conflicting prices on the same page** (C1, C2, C3): headline price differs
  from table price; same item has two prices.
- **Conflicting dates** (C1, C3, C5): title, body, and share text disagree.
- **No location**: every site uses "national average" or vague wording.
  Prices cannot be checked by a reader.
- **Unexplained "Verified" badges** (C1, C3, C4) with no method or date.
- **Mixed portions**: family-pan calories equal to single entree calories (C1);
  same item in Family-Style and Catering with different values (C4).
- **Unclear calorie basis**: ranges and unlabeled numbers; sauces sometimes
  added, sometimes not.
- **Count mismatches** (C4, C5): "158 items" vs. 210 in categories; "42 items"
  vs. ~38 listed.
- **Time-limited deals** that will be outdated within days.
- **Thin, repeated content**: same price lists in tables, cards, FAQ, calculators.
- **Fake-looking signals**: star ratings with 7-12 votes (C4).

## 5. What competitors do well (ideas, not copying)

- Short "at a glance" summary near the top.
- Jump links to sections.
- Clear category navigation.
- FAQ answering common practical questions.
- Visible "updated" date (when it is honest).
