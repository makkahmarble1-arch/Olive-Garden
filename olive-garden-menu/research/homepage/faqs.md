# Frequently Asked Questions (research draft)

**Checked:** 2026-11-12. Answers are written in our own words from official
pages and `research/menu/`. Prices are online pickup prices at Olive Garden
Orlando - Millenia Mall; they vary by location. Every answer is meant to be
shown on the page exactly as the FAQPage data says (no hidden FAQ text).

| # | Question | Answer (draft) | Sources |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | How much does a meal at Olive Garden cost? | At the Orlando Millenia Mall restaurant, classic entrées run about $13.99 to $26.49 for online pickup, lunch-size plates $11.49 to $12.49, and kids meals $7.49 to $9.98. Prices vary by location. | `research/menu/` (checked 2026-11-12) |
| 2 | Do entrées come with soup or salad and breadsticks? | Yes. Entrées, lunch plates, and lighter portions include a choice of soup or the Famous House Salad, plus breadsticks. Kids meals and $6 Take Home entrées do not. | Official item pages; take-home terms |
| 3 | When is the lunch menu served? | Monday through Friday, 11 AM to 3 PM. On weekends and holidays the dinner menu is served all day. Check your restaurant, since hours can vary. | Location page; official FAQ; specials page |
| 4 | Is Olive Garden open on Thanksgiving and Christmas? | No. Restaurants close on Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day, and may close early the day before Thanksgiving and on Christmas Eve, so call ahead. | Official FAQ |
| 5 | What is the $6 Take Home offer? | When you buy any entrée, you can add a chilled Fettuccine Alfredo, Five Cheese Ziti al Forno, or Spaghetti with Meat Sauce to heat later for $6. It comes without soup, salad, or breadsticks. | Official take-home offer page |
| 6 | Does Olive Garden have gluten-free food? | It has a gluten-sensitive menu made without gluten-containing ingredients, but those dishes are not certified gluten-free and there is no separate fryer. The gluten-free rotini meets the FDA gluten-free definition. | Official gluten-sensitive page |
| 7 | How many calories are in Olive Garden breadsticks? | Each breadstick with garlic topping has 140 calories (130 without the topping). An order of six is 840 calories. | Official nutrition PDF; 840 is calculated (6 × 140) |
| 8 | Can I order Olive Garden online for pickup or delivery? | Yes. Choose To Go or Catering on the official ordering page, pick your restaurant, then select pickup or delivery where offered. At the Millenia restaurant, online ordering closes 20 minutes before closing time. | Official order-online page; location page |
