## 3. How each value type was verified

- **Price:** read from the Millenia online ordering item page (S3).
  These are **online pickup** prices. Dine-in menu prices were not checked
  and may differ.
- **Calories:** read from the official nutrition PDF (S6) and compared with
  the calories shown on the ordering page (S2/S3).
- **Serving size:** only where the official item page states it (piece
  count, "serves N", oz). Otherwise "Not verified".
- **Availability:** "Listed" = shown on the Millenia ordering menu on
  2026-11-11. It does not prove in-restaurant availability on any given day.
