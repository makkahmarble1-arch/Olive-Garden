## 2. Dipping Sauces: values and the conflict

Official PDF (page 3), per sauce:

| Sauce | Regular | Large |
|---|---|---|
| Marinara | 70 | 150 |
| Five Cheese Marinara | 200 | 400 |
| Alfredo | 440 | 870 |
| Spicy Alfredo | 490 | 980 |

Plus breadsticks at 140 cal each (regular comes with 4, large with 8).

Conflict: the ordering page card says regular sauces are "90 - 440 cal" and
large "190 - 870 cal". The PDF says 70-490 and 150-980. The item's own
"View Nutritional Info" panel matches the PDF. The ordering page did not
clearly show whether Spicy Alfredo is offered at Millenia.
**Not published until the owner decides** which basis to show.

## 3. Note on the "View Nutritional Info" panels

On the ordering item pages, the "View Nutritional Info" panel sometimes shows
a single combined number that seems to include the sauces (for example
Stuffed Ziti Fritta 760 = 500 + 35 + 220 rounded; Shrimp Fritto Misto 1,560;
Toasted Ravioli 680). This is an interpretation, not stated on the page.
The table above uses the PDF's per-component values, which also match the
menu cards. Do not use the combined panel numbers on the site.
